--------------------------------------------------
1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW & EXPORT ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------
AptaSoil integrates real-time NASA Earth Observation telemetry (SMAP, MODIS, POWER) with in-silico DNA aptamer binding kinetics. To facilitate seamless reporting, submission, and offline research, the platform includes robust client-side export capabilities for both PDF summary reports and raw CSV telemetry datasets.
--------------------------------------------------
2. STEP-BY-STEP OPERATION & EXPORT WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Dashboard Initialization & Region Selection
- Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.
- Select your target agricultural zone using the Leaflet GIS map presets (e.g., Punjab, Andalusia, Midwest, Cerrado) or click directly on the map to poll Nominatim geocoding and live NASA APIs.
Step 2: Real-Time Telemetry & SFI Analysis
- Review the live Soil Fatigue Index (SFI) calculation, which fuses phenolic toxicity (Ferulic Acid), canopy health (NDVI), and root-zone soil moisture (NASA SMAP).
- Evaluate the 3D molecular aptamer dock view and the dynamic 30+ crop rotation registry.
Step 3: Exporting Professional PDF Mission Reports
- Locate the "Export PDF Report" button on the interface.
- Clicking this triggers the client-side jsPDF and AutoTable generation engine.
- The exported PDF includes:
- Mission Header: Team Mobotics & NASA Space Apps Challenge metadata.
- Location Coordinates & Environmental Telemetry (SMAP moisture, MODIS NDVI, POWER solar/temp).
- Computed SFI Score & Fatigue Status.
- Recommended Crop Rotation & Remediation Protocols.
Step 4: Exporting Raw CSV Telemetry Data
- Locate the "Export CSV" or telemetry log download button.
- Generates a comma-separated values file containing timestamped sensor nodes, Ferulic Acid concentrations (uM), moisture percentages, NDVI values, and SFI scores for offline data science and research modeling.
